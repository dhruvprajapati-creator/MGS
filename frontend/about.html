Who we are

Trusted Field Verification
Since 2005

Maya Global Services Pvt. Ltd. was established in 2005 with a simple objective—to provide financial institutions with dependable, transparent, and accurate field verification services.

Over the past two decades, we have grown into one of North India's trusted verification partners, serving leading Banks, NBFCs, Housing Finance Companies, FinTechs, Insurance Companies, and Corporate Organizations.

Today, with a network of 445+ trained field executives and technology-enabled operations, we conduct thousands of verification assignments while helping clients reduce operational risk and make informed lending decisions.
