Frequently Asked Questions

What is your typical turnaround time (TAT)?

expand_more

Our standard TAT is just 4 hours for most field verification reports. For bulk orders or complex cases, TAT may vary. We provide real-time status updates through our MIS dashboard.

Which regions do you cover?

expand_more

We currently cover Rajasthan, Haryana, Punjab, Gurgaon, and Delhi NCR. See our coverage page for more details.

How do you ensure data security?

expand_more

We employ bank-grade encryption for all data transmission and storage. Our field agents use a secured mobile application, and all documents are handled with strict access controls and audit trails.

What types of verification services do you provide?

expand_more

We offer a comprehensive suite of services including Field Investigation, Customer Verification, Risk Management, Account Origination, Digital MIS Reporting, and Post Disbursement Verification. See our services page for more details.

Are your field executives directly verified and tracked?

expand_more

Yes, our network of over 445+ field executives are thoroughly background-checked and extensively trained. They utilize our proprietary mobile application to capture real-time data, ensuring 100% accurate and tamper-proof reporting.
